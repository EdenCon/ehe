Every moment with you feels like home, A place where my heart is never alone. Your laughter lingers, soft and clear, And every whisper draws me near. The stars may fall, the night may fade, But your warmth is a light that will never jade. Your hand in mine, a perfect fit, A silent promise, a gentle knit. I watch your eyes, deep oceans of calm, Every glance from you feels like a balm. The world spins fast, yet here I stay, Anchored to you, come what may. Each word you speak, a melody sweet, Each heartbeat of yours makes mine complete. In storms or sunshine, in joy or fear, My home is your heart, always near. I’ll hold you close, through all life’s miles, Finding eternity in your soft smiles. Time cannot steal what we have grown, For every moment with you feels like home. With every hug, the world feels right, Your love is my shelter in darkest night. Even when silence surrounds the air, I feel your heartbeat always there. No treasure compares, no gold could shine, Like the quiet magic of calling you mine. Forever isn’t long enough to show, How much my heart loves you, you know. I find my peace in the way you stay, In simple moments, day by day. You turn the ordinary into something true, Just by being you, just by being you. When the world feels loud and pulls me away, Your presence brings me back to okay. You ground my thoughts, you calm my mind, A steady comfort I didn’t know I’d find. Every step feels lighter when you’re near, Every doubt fades, every fear. You don’t have to try or prove a thing, Your love is enough, it’s everything. In crowded rooms or quiet space, I search for you, your familiar face. Because wherever you happen to be, That’s where home feels like to me. I don’t need walls or perfect plans, Just your warmth and your steady hands. Wherever life decides to roam, If I’m with you, I’m already home. So if the world should change its tone, And paths feel unsure, unknown, I’ll follow the light I’ve always known— Home has always been your smile alone.
Some days feel gray before I wake, The world is heavy, my heart aches. I walk the halls with quiet steps, Lost in thoughts, in lonely depths. The sky outside is pale and dim, My mind is heavy, my chances slim. I barely notice the world around, Until your presence is finally found. Then I see you, just standing there, Your quiet smile, your gentle stare. The clouds inside me start to fade, A little light begins to invade. Even a glance can shift my mood, Turn lonely silence into something good. A passing word, a fleeting grin, And suddenly I feel warmth within. The minutes stretch, the hallways slow, Yet time feels faster when I see you go. I catch your eyes in the crowded room, And all at once, the gray turns bloom. The day feels lighter, the halls less cold, Your presence bright, a sight to behold. I don’t need hours or grand displays, Just a spark from you to light my days. Even small moments, a shared glance, Make my heart skip, give it a chance. A scribbled note, a whispered “hi,” Can chase the storm clouds from the sky. Rain may fall, and skies may weep, My thoughts of you run soft and deep. Though life feels heavy, my heart confined, Seeing you makes the sun shine behind. The world goes on, the day drifts by, But your gentle light will never die. Even brief moments, though few and small, Bring me a warmth that outshines all. I leave the school, the hallways bare, Yet carry your light with me everywhere. Though the clouds return and skies grow gray, I’ll remember your sun throughout the day. You are the spark that breaks the gloom, The gentle warmth that fills the room. Even in sadness, when hope seems thin, You shine inside me, bright within. Some days may start with sorrow’s haze, Yet you arrive, my sun on rainy days. A quiet presence, soft and true, I feel the world is bright with you.
In quiet moments, when the world feels slow, When voices fade and lights hang low, I find my thoughts drifting to you, To all the small things you say and do. The noise around me starts to thin, And that’s where your presence slips in, Not loud or bold, just calm and clear, Like something meant to linger here. I think of you between the days, In empty halls and quiet ways, When nothing asks for my attention, But you still hold it without intention. There’s comfort in the stillness there, In knowing someone else exists somewhere, Someone who makes the silence kind, Someone my heart seems trained to find. I replay moments, small and brief, A passing look, a quiet relief, The way the day feels less unsure, Just knowing you’re somewhere near. You don’t have to speak or stay, Your presence changes things anyway, Like peace arriving without a sound, Like calm where chaos once was found. In moments where I feel too much, Your memory feels like a gentle touch, A steady thought I hold onto, When everything else feels untrue. Sometimes I wonder if you know, How quietly my feelings grow, How even distance doesn’t break The comfort your existence makes. I don’t need promises or plans, Just shared moments, unspoken stands, Where nothing’s forced and nothing’s said, But something real lives in my head. The quiet doesn’t scare me now, Not when I know you’re around somehow, Even unseen, even apart, You take up space inside my heart. In stillness, I find clarity, A softer version of reality, Where thinking of you feels like rest, And the noise feels less intense. If quiet moments had a sound, It’d be your name, soft and profound, A gentle echo, warm and true, That always seems to lead to you. So when the world grows loud again, And days rush forward without end, I’ll keep these moments, small and deep, The quiet thoughts of you I keep.
